# Peaceinthemiddleeast
Privacy Policy for Peace in the Middle East

  Effective Date: May 3, 2026

  The "Peace in the Middle East" project (referred to as "we," "us," or "the Project") is committed to
  protecting the privacy of its contributors, users, and community members. This Privacy Policy
  explains how we handle data and your rights regarding that information.

  1. Information We Collect
  We strive to collect the minimum amount of data necessary to maintain the Project.

   * Public Contributions: Any information you contribute to this repository (e.g., issues, pull
     requests, comments) is public by design. Your GitHub username and profile information will be
     associated with these contributions as per GitHub's own terms.
   * Technical Data: When you visit our website (hosted via GitHub Pages), GitHub may automatically
     collect visitor logs, including IP addresses, to maintain the security and integrity of the
     service.
   * Voluntary Information: If you contact us directly via email or provide information through a
     community form, we collect only the information you choose to share.

  2. How We Use Information
  We use the collected information solely for:
   * Maintaining and improving the Project's resources.
   * Facilitating community discussion and collaboration.
   * Ensuring the security of our hosting platform.

  We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

  3. Third-Party Services
  This Project utilizes the following third-party services:
   * GitHub / GitHub Pages: Our primary hosting and collaboration platform. Their privacy practices are
     governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement
     (https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement).
   * (Optional: List other services like Google Analytics, Discord, or Slack if applicable)

  4. Cookies and Tracking
   * This Project does not use its own tracking cookies.
   * Third-party platforms (like GitHub) may use cookies for essential functionality. You can manage
     cookie preferences through your browser settings.

  5. Community Safety and Security
  Given the nature of this Project, we prioritize a safe environment for all. While contributions are
  public, we encourage users to avoid sharing sensitive personal identification (such as home addresses
  or private phone numbers) in public threads.

  6. Your Rights
  Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to:
   * Request a copy of any data we hold about you.
   * Request that we delete your personal data (though public GitHub history may be subject to GitHub's
     own data retention policies).
   * Withdraw consent for any voluntary data processing.

  7. Changes to This Policy
  We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes will be announced via the
  repository's "Announcements" or through a commit message to this file.

  8. Contact Us
  If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please:
